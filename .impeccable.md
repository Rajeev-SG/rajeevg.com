# Design Context

## Users

- Primary audience: potential collaborators, employers, clients, and technically curious readers visiting rajeevg.com to understand Rajeev Gill's work.
- Main jobs: understand what Rajeev builds, inspect selected projects, read useful writing, and find a clear way to get in touch.
- Visitor context: most people arrive without knowing the site's internal publishing structure and should be able to orient themselves within a few seconds.

## Brand Personality

- Practical, candid, and assured.
- Plain spoken and evidence led, with technical depth available when a reader chooses it.
- The experience should create confidence and curiosity without sounding promotional or explaining its own information architecture.

## Aesthetic Direction

- An understated editorial portfolio: strong typography, generous spacing, real project imagery, and a small number of deliberate accents.
- Support both light and dark themes.
- Prefer page composition, lists, and editorial rhythm over repeated card grids and status badges.
- Preserve the site's restrained neutral palette while avoiding neon AI styling, decorative gradients, and dense application-style navigation.

## Design Principles

1. Lead with Rajeev and the work, never with the publishing system behind the site.
2. Every public section must help a visitor understand, inspect, read, or contact.
3. Internal workflow fields, unpublished ideas, route verification details, and editorial instructions stay private.
4. Use cards only when the whole object is a meaningful interaction, such as a selected project.
5. Keep navigation small and predictable: Home, Projects, Writing, and About.
6. Make the first mobile viewport useful, with clear identity and a reachable action.
7. Maintain readable contrast, keyboard access, reduced-motion support, and at least 44px touch targets.
