# Design context

- Audience: beginners and technically curious Apple Silicon Mac users who want to understand or reproduce a local Qwen setup in Codex.
- Job: explain the moving parts, the supported model-selector configuration, the role of each local service, and the limits of images, tools, and context compression.
- Voice: first person, candid, calm, plain spoken, and evidence led. Explain every acronym on first use.
- Visual direction: an editorial technical field guide that matches the existing rajeevg.com build-log articles. Use real screenshots, diagrams, terminal-style proof, generous spacing, and a restrained slate, amber, sky, and emerald palette.
- Avoid: neon AI styling, generic card grids, vague promotional language, unexplained jargon, and instructions that modify the signed ChatGPT application bundle.
- Responsive requirement: diagrams, screenshots, code, and comparison panels must remain readable at 430px, 1440px, and 1575px widths.
