Performance Max audience signals

Optional audience suggestions you attach to a Performance Max asset group. They tell Google’s AI who to look for first. They do not limit who sees your ads: Performance Max will serve to people outside your signals when it predicts they are likely to convert.

What you can give it

Your dataWebsite visitors, app users, customer lists, video viewers. Usually the strongest signal.
Custom segmentsSearch terms, competitor or similar URLs, app names that describe your buyer.
Google segmentsAffinity, in-market, life events and detailed demographics.
DemographicsAge, gender, parental status — narrows the signal, still not a restriction.

Use it when

  • The campaign is new or has little conversion history — signals shorten the learning period.
  • You have good first-party lists (customers, high-value visitors).
  • Different asset groups target genuinely different buyers.

Don’t rely on it when

  • You must keep ads away from an audience — signals won’t do that.
  • You want to read audience reports as “who we targeted”. Delivery goes wider.
  • You need strict audience targeting — use a Search, Demand Gen or Display campaign.

Set it up

  1. Open the Performance Max campaign and go to Asset groups.
  2. Edit the asset group and find the Audience signal section below the assets.
  3. Choose an existing audience, or create one: name it, then add your data and custom segments.
  4. Optionally add Google segments and demographics, then Save. Repeat per asset group.

What it can move

Time to stable CPA / ROASFaster, because the AI starts near likely converters.
Early-flight efficiencyLess spend wasted on exploration in the first weeks.
Reach and audience mixNot capped. Expect delivery outside the signal over time.
